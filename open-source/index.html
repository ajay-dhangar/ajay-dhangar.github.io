THE CONTRIBUTION MINDSET

Small improvements.
Meaningful progress.

Open source is not just about writing code. It is also about documentation, communication, maintenance, and helping other people participate.

01

Explore & understand

Read existing code, understand the issue, review project guidelines, and identify a useful improvement.

02

Build & improve

Make focused changes, write clear documentation, test the result, and keep the solution maintainable.

03

Share & collaborate

Open a pull request, explain the change, welcome feedback, and learn from maintainers and fellow contributors.
